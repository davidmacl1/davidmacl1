## David Levy

I'm a fitness practitioner who builds production AI systems. I've coached people for 30+ years, many of them coming back from injury or living with chronic issues, and I've run my own coaching company since 2008. Now I build AI that thinks the way an expert practitioner does, and I ship it to real users with Claude Code.

### What I've built

**Archimedes**: a platform that turns a practitioner's methodology into AI tools: *AI that thinks like you do.*
- A structured AI interview captures how an expert thinks and turns it into an ontology that configures their AI.
- **Fitness:** Archimedes Coach and Archimedes Train, native iOS, Android and web apps with six specialized AI agents (each built on its own ontology), client memory, intake-based routing, progress tracking, assessments, invoicing and scheduling.
- **Veterinary:** clinical tools used by integrative vets in the US and New Zealand. They write SOAP notes from appointment audio, plus follow-ups and lab recheck reminders in each vet's voice.
- Stack: React, React Native (Expo), Node, Supabase, Claude API, Stripe, Vercel, Railway.

**[practitioner-ontology-interviewer](https://github.com/davidmacl1/practitioner-ontology-interviewer)**: the open-source core of that interview process, with evals that check whether an expert's voice, guardrails and open items survive extraction. It includes a real regression the evals caught and the fix.

**Business automation** for my own company, running daily:
- A lead bot that texts new Meta leads real appointment times, follows up, and passes anything unusual to staff (GoHighLevel + Meta APIs).
- An AI video ad pipeline: Render-generated ads deployed through the Meta Ads API to chosen audiences and budgets across a multi-stage funnel.
- Weekly KPI reporting that reconciles billing and CRM data, insurance claims prep, and inbox routing.
